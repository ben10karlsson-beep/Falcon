# Falcon

A poker study app for understanding the math and strategy of poker, pre and post flop.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in any browser. There is no build step and nothing to install.

## What's in it

- **Preflop ranges** – 13×13 range grids for 6-max cash games (100bb), covering opening ranges for each seat and five common spots facing a raise. Click any hand to see how it is played and why.
- **Trainer** – deals random hands and seats and grades your decisions against the charts. "My leaks" mode deals more of the spots you get wrong and replays hands you missed. Progress is saved in the browser.
- **Theory** – pot odds, counting outs, combinations and blockers, preflop matchups, position and GTO basics, with interactive calculators.

## Notes

The ranges are simplified approximations, hand-built to match the shape of solver strategies. They are not exact solver output.
