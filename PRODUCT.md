## Platform

web

## Users
Players teaching themselves 6-max No-Limit Hold'em cash games (100bb), studying at home between sessions on a laptop or phone. They want to understand why a play is right (equity, ranges, combos, solver frequencies), not just memorise charts. (Confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-14.)

## Product Purpose
Falcon is a free poker study app: preflop range charts, graded trainers (preflop hands, reading ranges, hand vs hand, range trainer, c-bet), an equity calculator, a board explorer with solver output for BTN vs BB flops, and a theory course of seven chapters. Every tool explains its reasoning in plain language. Success is a player who can explain their own decisions with the math behind them.

## Positioning
Every number is computed live by Falcon's own engine or comes from its own solver runs, and every recommendation comes with the reason. Charts are honest approximations and say so. It is a study companion that teaches, not a lookup table.
